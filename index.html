SERVICES

A flexible partner for event projects.

PULPO supports event organisers, agencies and businesses through operational coordination, event technology and on-site expertise.

From preparation to execution, we adapt to your needs and become an extension of your team.

The approach is flexible, hands-on and solution-oriented.

EVENT PRODUCTION & OPERATIONS

Major events, international events and agencies: we support organisers in coordinating their operations and integrating new technology solutions.

  • Event operations coordination
  • Supporting organisers as their set-up evolves
  • Organisational integration of technology solutions
  • Coordination between organising teams and technical providers
  • Change management and adoption of new tools
  • Operational supervision and crowd flow management

We work on the organiser's side, within their team: the link between the organisation and its technical providers.

CORPORATE & BRAND EXPERIENCES

We support companies, agencies and organisations in preparing and coordinating their corporate events.

  • Business events and meetings
  • Seminars
  • Brand experiences
  • Internal events
  • Overall coordination, supplier management and participant experience

SPORT, OUTDOOR & LIVE EXPERIENCES

THE FOUNDER

I'm Margot Alcaras, founder of PULPO.

My experience spans major international events, sports and outdoor projects, corporate events and event technology.

Over the years, I've developed a cross-disciplinary vision of event production: understanding organisational challenges, anticipating on-site constraints, bringing the right people together and turning ideas into meaningful experiences.

I created PULPO to offer a different approach to event production: flexible, human and adaptable to the diversity of projects.

Working alongside organisers, connecting expertise and supporting projects from concept to delivery.

PULPO is the drive not to be limited to one format, one sector or one way of doing things.
